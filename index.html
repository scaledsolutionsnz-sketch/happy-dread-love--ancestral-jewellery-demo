Since 2021 in Raglan

Five years in Whaingaroa

Happy Dread Love began as a family caravan studio and has been part of Raglan for five years. Venus looks after the dread work with a gentle, holistic approach: your scalp and hair health come first, and every session is unhurried.

The jewellery side is led by artist Dagoberto Navia, known as Pájaro Veloz Newen. He shapes pounamu, sterling silver, copper and gemstones free-hand into one-of-a-kind talismans. You can also find pieces at Well Read on Wallis Street, in the heart of Raglan.

  • Family owned and run in Raglan
  • Gentle work that protects your hair
  • Every jewel one of a kind, made by hand
